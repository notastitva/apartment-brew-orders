Hot Extracted. Instantly Flash-Chilled. Our Manifesto

We operate as an asset-light, pre-order micro-roastery in Gurugram. Instead of mass-producing coffee in giant commercial tanks to sit on supermarket shelves for months, we brew each coffee separately by hand in small batches specifically for scheduled weekly drops.

“We don't make shelf-stable concentrates. We extract single-estate specialty coffee hot, flash-chill it over ice to lock in volatile aromatics, and deliver it fresh within a strict 48-hour peak window.”

Why The Apartment Brew Co. Is Different
🔥 1. Hot Extraction • Thermal Flash-Chill
Coffee needs heat (92–94°C) to dissolve sweet fruit sugars and delicate floral aromatic oils. We extract hot and flash-chill the brew over ice to 4°C in seconds, permanently trapping wild berry and citrus aromatics before oxidation can begin.
🌱 2. Single-Estate Farm Direct Beans
We roast 100% specialty arabica sourced directly from partner farms in the Western Ghats (including Ratnagiri Estate Anaerobic Naturals). No cheap robusta, no artificial extracts, and no dark bitter roasts.
❄ 3. 100% Preservative Free (48-Hr Peak)
Zero chemical stabilizers, zero pasteurization, and zero additives. Bottled fresh overnight for scheduled weekend and Friday office drops. Keep refrigerated and enjoy poured over ice within 48 hours for pure cafe pour-over quality.
✋ 4. Artisanal Nuance in Every Bottle
Every order is brewed manually by hand rather than automated commercial machines. Slight cup-to-cup character is celebrated as a deliberate hallmark of manual craft extraction.
