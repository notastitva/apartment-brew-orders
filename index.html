The Craft Philosophy Why We Are Different
“We don't brew shelf-stable supermarket concentrates. We extract single-estate specialty coffee hot to dissolve volatile aromatics, flash-chill them instantly over ice, and deliver them fresh within 48 hours.”
🔥 1. Hot Extraction and Thermal Flash-Chill
Coffee needs heat (92–94°C) to dissolve sweet fruit sugars and delicate floral aromatic oils. We extract hot and instantly flash-chill the brew over ice to 4°C in seconds, permanently locking in volatile aromatics before oxidation can begin.
🌱 2. Single-Estate Farm Direct Arabica
We roast 100% specialty grade arabica sourced directly from partner farms in the Western Ghats (including Ratnagiri Estate Anaerobic Naturals). Zero robusta, zero artificial extracts, and zero dark burnt roasts.
❄️ 3. 100% Preservative Free (48-Hr Peak Rule)
Zero chemical stabilizers, zero industrial pasteurization, and zero additives. Bottled fresh overnight in premium 200ml glass bottles. Keep refrigerated and enjoy poured over ice within 48 hours for pure cafe pour-over quality.
🖐️ 4. Artisanal Nuance in Every Bottle
Every batch is brewed separately by hand rather than pumped out of automated mass-market tanks. Slight cup-to-cup character is celebrated as a deliberate hallmark of manual specialty extraction.

Frequently Asked Questions Everything You Need to Know
Why flash-chill over ice instead of traditional cold brew? +
Traditional cold brew steeps coffee in cold water for 18–24 hours. While smooth and low-acid, it fails to dissolve the delicate aromatic oils and fruit acids that give light-to-medium specialty coffees their signature berry and floral notes. We extract hot (92–94°C) to capture the full flavor spectrum, then flash-chill immediately to 4°C to lock in those aromatics before oxidation occurs.
How long does the coffee stay fresh? +
Because our coffee has zero preservatives, zero chemical stabilizers, and zero pasteurization, it is designed to be enjoyed within 48 hours of delivery. Keep your 200ml glass bottles refrigerated below 4°C upon arrival and pour over clean ice for peak flavor.
Where do you deliver across Delhi NCR? +
We operate scheduled cold-chain delivery drops across Gurugram (DLF Phase 1–5, Cyber City, Cyber Hub, Golf Course Road, Golf Course Extension, Sector 48, Candor TechSpace, Udyog Vihar), South and Central Delhi, and Noida (Sectors 62, 126, 135).
How do the order cutoffs and delivery slots work? +
For weekend residential drops, Saturday deliveries close Friday at 10:00 PM, and Sunday deliveries close Saturday at 10:00 PM (both delivered 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM). For Friday corporate office drops, orders close Thursday at 6:00 PM with Morning Kickoff (9:30–11:30 AM) and Afternoon Recharge (2:00–4:00 PM) delivery windows.
What bottle format and sizes do you use? +
Every order across all consumer packs, corporate tiers, and event batches is standardized into 200ml premium glass bottles. Glass preserves the delicate fruit acidity and aroma without leaching chemicals or altering flavor.
