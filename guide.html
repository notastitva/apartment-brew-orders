The Flash-Chilling Science

Most ready-to-drink bottled coffees are standard 18-hour cold brew immersion or pasteurized supermarket concentrates. While smooth, cold immersion fails to extract the volatile fruity acids and floral aromatics inherent in light-to-medium specialty roasts.

We extract hot (92–94°C) to capture the complete sensory spectrum of single-estate beans, and then immediately flash-chill the coffee over ice, rapidly dropping its temperature to 4°C. This thermal shock traps volatile flavor molecules before they escape into the air.

The 48-Hour Peak Window

Because our coffee has zero preservatives, zero stabilizers, and zero pasteurization, its delicate flavor peak is active for 48 hours from dispatch:

Hours 0–12 (Drop Day): Bright, punchy aromatics, vibrant berry and citrus acidity, and crisp finish.
Hours 12–36 (Peak Roundness): Acidity integrates smoothly with chocolate and stone fruit undertones.
Hours 36–48 (Mellow Finish): Deeper body and rounded cacao sweetness. Consume within 48 hours!
Storage & Serving Ritual
1. Immediate Cold Storage: Transfer your 250ml glass bottles to your refrigerator (below 4°C) as soon as delivered.
2. Serve Over Clean Ice: Pour 125–150ml of chilled coffee over dense, clear ice cubes in a short glass.
3. Swirl & Savor: Let the coffee aerate for 30 seconds to allow the floral bouquet to bloom. Enjoy pure black with zero sugar.
